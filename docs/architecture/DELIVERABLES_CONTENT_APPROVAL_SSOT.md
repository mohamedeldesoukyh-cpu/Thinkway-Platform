## What remains valid

These boundaries from the earlier lock are **still normative**:

```text
Assignment → Deliverable → Publication → Performance
```

- Draft / documentation assets are **never** Publications  
- Deliverables **never** own Performance metrics  
- After go-live, Deliverable may hold a **reference only** to Publication  

---

## What changed

Product clarified that Deliverables is **not** a workflow / approval engine.

It is the **Documentation & Asset Repository** SSOT:

- Files, links, captions, copy, notes, versions, comments, audit  
- Answers: “What content and documentation have we received?”  
- Media Plan shows documentation completeness via creator avatar indicators (visual only)

Internal / client / creator **comments** live on the repository. Formal campaign/assignment workflow and publishing stay outside Deliverables.
